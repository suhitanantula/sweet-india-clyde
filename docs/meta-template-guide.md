# Meta Template Approval Guide

*How to get WhatsApp message templates approved first time.*

---

## The Rules

Meta reviews every message template before it can be sent. Templates that look like spam get rejected. Templates that look like a real business talking to real customers get approved.

### What Meta Wants
- Clear, specific content (not generic "check out our deals!")
- Business name visible in the message
- No deceptive or pressure tactics
- Opt-out option (even though WhatsApp handles this)
- Correct category (Marketing, Utility, or Authentication)

### What Meta Rejects
- Too many emojis or excessive formatting
- Urgency language ("HURRY!", "LAST CHANCE!!!", "DON'T MISS OUT")
- Vague or generic content
- Misleading subject lines or previews
- Content that doesn't match the template category

## Approval Timeline

- **First submission:** 24–48 hours
- **Resubmission after rejection:** 12–24 hours
- **Best practice:** Submit 5+ variants, use the ones that get approved

## If Rejected

Common rejection reasons and fixes:
1. **"Content is too promotional"** → Tone it down, remove emojis, be more informative
2. **"Missing opt-out"** → Add "Reply STOP to unsubscribe" (rarely required but helps)
3. **"Category mismatch"** → Make sure marketing templates are filed as Marketing
4. **"Unclear value"** → Be specific about products, prices, and offers
